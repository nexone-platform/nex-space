# Office theme art credits

This folder mixes two sources with **different licences**. Check the prefix
before reusing a file.

## `cs-*` — CoolSchool tileset

- **Artist:** NettySvit
- **License:** [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) —
  public domain, no attribution required (the credit here is courtesy)
- Desks, desktop computers and chairs, cut from `CoolSchool_tileset.png`

These are **48px** art placed at native size, so a desk covers 3x3 of the map's
32px tiles. They are deliberately not resampled: 48 -> 32 is a x0.667 downscale
and would break the pixel grid. Their chairs and computers are used alongside
them so everything on a workstation shares one pixel scale.

## everything else — LPC "The Office"

- **Artists:** Eliza Wyatt, Lanea Zimmerman (Sharm)
- **License:** [OGA-BY 3.0](https://static.opengameart.org/OGA-BY-3.0.txt)
  (CC-BY 3.0 with a DRM waiver)
- **Source:** https://github.com/ElizaWy/LPC

`credenza`, `cabinet`, `desk`, laptops, coffee cup/maker, phone, copier, water
cooler, mailboxes, bins, card tables, TV and portraits. Native 32px.

`Desk, Ornate` was made by Eliza Wyatt using pieces of Lanea Zimmerman's
cupboards and countertops; the DRM waiver on those pieces is Lanea's.

### What OGA-BY obliges us to do

Unlike the CC0 art here, OGA-BY **requires attribution wherever the game is
published**. Surface this credit in the app before shipping the theme, and keep
it there while these files are in use.

Sprites were only cut apart and renamed; the pixels are unmodified.
