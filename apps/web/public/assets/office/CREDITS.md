# Office theme art credits

The props in this folder are cut from the **LPC "The Office"** set.

- **Artists:** Eliza Wyatt, Lanea Zimmerman (Sharm)
- **License:** [OGA-BY 3.0](https://static.opengameart.org/OGA-BY-3.0.txt)
  (CC-BY 3.0 with a DRM waiver)
- **Source:** https://github.com/ElizaWy/LPC

`Desk, Ornate` was made by Eliza Wyatt using pieces of Lanea Zimmerman's
cupboards and countertops; the DRM waiver on those pieces is Lanea's.

## What this obliges us to do

OGA-BY 3.0 is **not** public domain — unlike the CC0 art elsewhere in this
project, it **requires attribution wherever the game is published**. The credit
above is surfaced in the app (see the settings panel in the sidebar), so it
ships with any build. Do not remove it while these files are in use.

Sprites were only cut apart and renamed; the pixels are unmodified.
